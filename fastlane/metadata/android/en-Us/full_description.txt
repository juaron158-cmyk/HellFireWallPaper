HellFire Wallpaper is a live wallpaper that turns your screen into a fire, rain or snow scene, with optional layers that grow over the image.

Main features:
- Fire, rain and snow modes, with adjustable color, height, smoke, wind and speed.
- Snow piles that build up on the bottom of the screen and are cleared over time.
- Frost layer and crystal ferns that grow from the edges toward the center.
- Growth patterns (dendrites, maze, coral and others) and Lichtenberg-style lightning rays.
- Custom background image, with cropping.
- Fine-tune grain size, brightness and transparency.
- Optional gyroscope control for wind.
- Interface in English and Spanish.

Privacy:
- The notification reaction only checks which app sent a notification. It does not read notification content.
- No account is needed.
- The app only requires permissions to access images for use as a wallpaper. The permissions requested are:
 android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
 android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER
 android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
 android.permission.BIND_WALLPAPER

This app is in beta. Some effects may be heavier on older devices; lower the grain size or the effect intensity if the animation is slow.
